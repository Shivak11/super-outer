# Source authority and benchmark evidence

Inspect only the minimum relevant sources and classify their authority without
inflation.

1. **Binding or normative requirements:** applicable law, regulation,
   contractual security/privacy/data-integrity duties, accessibility standards,
   and normative protocol specifications.
2. **Explicit company and repository contracts:** source-linked facts marked
   `EXPLICIT` in the active profile, including product promises and local design
   or operating systems.
3. **Official platform guidance:** current documentation and runtime behavior
   from the responsible platform, framework, protocol, or maintainer.
4. **Evidence-backed engineering practice:** primary empirical research or a
   mature, relevant reference implementation with stated limitations.
5. **Observed local precedent:** code, tests, history, or runtime behavior
   marked `OBSERVED`; useful evidence, never silent policy.
6. **External stylistic precedent:** admissible only when local product
   direction is absent, and never a source of company identity.

For every cited finding record the class, source/version date when available,
inspection date, applicability, trade-off or limitation, local agreement or
conflict, and confidence. A blog, popularity, search rank, or personal taste is
not a recognised standard. If current external verification is unavailable,
state the limitation and lower confidence.

Remove private names, code, content, prompts, credentials, and unpublished
product logic from public research queries. Use local or approved private
sources when a safe generic query cannot answer the question.
