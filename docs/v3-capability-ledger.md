# OUTER v3 capability ledger

Inventory source: an isolated read-only snapshot of the installed OUTER v3
skill, inspected 2026-08-03. This ledger maps behavior, not filenames. `Preserved`
means the contract remains; `Generalised` means v4 broadens or strengthens it;
`Repository-layered` means global/plugin guidance remains but enforcement or
evidence also belongs to the participating repository; `Deprecated` means the
v3 mechanism is intentionally replaced. No row authorizes v4 promotion or v3
removal.

## Deliberate deprecations

Three v3 mechanisms are deliberately deprecated: the old long skill-name
invocations, the old approval phrase, and the long pending lifetime. Their
replacements retain `/outer`, use the native Super-outer skill, and shorten
approval. OUTER 2.1 and v3 remain recoverable in separate hash-verified archives
after v4 passes isolated, cross-host, exact-head verification; neither earlier
version remains discoverable.
