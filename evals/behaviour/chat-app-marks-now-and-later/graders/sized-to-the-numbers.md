PASS if the plan puts live message delivery and limits against spam or abuse (such as limits on sign-ups, OTPs or messages per minute) in what is needed now, puts caching or extra database copies in later with a stated trigger, and does not plan sharding, multiple regions or a self-managed server fleet for launch.

FAIL if it plans sharding, multi-region or self-managed load balancing from day one, or leaves out abuse limits, or gives later items no trigger.
